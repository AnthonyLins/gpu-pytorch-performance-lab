## Short version

GPU and PyTorch Performance Lab: **(https://github.com/AnthonyLins/gpu-pytorch-performance-lab)**

A reproducible Google Colab study comparing PyTorch eager execution, `torch.compile`, and a custom Triton softmax kernel. It includes numerical validation, synchronized CUDA benchmarks, environment metadata, percentile latency, effective-bandwidth estimates, and a PyTorch Profiler trace.
